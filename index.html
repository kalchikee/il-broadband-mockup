⚡ Why Broadband Access Matters
🎓 Students in low-coverage areas lack access to online coursework and digital libraries, widening educational inequity.
💼 Rural workers without reliable broadband are excluded from remote job opportunities and online business tools.
🏥 Telehealth services — critical for rural communities far from hospitals — require minimum 25 Mbps to function reliably.
💰 Federal BEAD Program funding (~$42.5B) requires data-driven prioritization. This tool identifies where investment has the greatest impact.

ℹ️ About This Tool

Purpose

The Illinois Broadband Gap Analysis Tool identifies and visualizes broadband service gaps across 3,263 Illinois census tracts by combining ACS internet subscription data with Census demographic indicators. It supports data-driven infrastructure investment decisions for state broadband planners, grant writers, and community advocates.

Who Is This For?

Infrastructure PlannersIdentify highest-need areas and prioritize capital investment.
Grant WritersGenerate data for BEAD / USDA ReConnect funding applications.
Community AdvocatesCommunicate the digital divide to local officials.
ResearchersAnalyze spatial patterns in digital equity across Illinois.

Broadband Need Index Methodology

Composite index (0–100) combining three normalized components. Higher scores = greater infrastructure investment need.

40%
Broadband coverage gap (100% minus broadband rate)
30%
Income gap (normalized over $20k–$120k range)
30%
No internet rate (% households without any internet)

Data Sources

ACS American Community Survey 5-Year 2024 — internet subscriptions, income, population
FCC Broadband Data Collection — county-level coverage (January 2025)
Census TIGER/Line 2023 — Illinois census tract boundaries
